## Domain analysis and decisions

### Content scope and urgent preservation (`content_scope`)

Identify records, working copies, source repositories, and content needing immediate preservation.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Taxonomy and content model (`taxonomy`)

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Capture and publication (`capture`)

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Access and retrieval (`access_search`)

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Retention rules (`retention`)

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Legal hold and chain of custody (`legal_hold`)

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Disposition and retirement (`disposition`)

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Migration and audit acceptance (`acceptance_evidence`)

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.
