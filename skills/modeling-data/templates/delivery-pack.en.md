## Domain analysis and decisions

### Business use cases and scope (`use_cases`)

State the business processes, decisions, and consumers supported by the model.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Model level and approach (`model_level`)

Choose conceptual, logical, or physical level and the appropriate relational, dimensional, or other pattern.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Requirements and terms (`requirements`)

List governed definitions, disputed terms, and approval status of business rules.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Grain keys and relationships (`grain_keys`)

Fix entity or fact grain, keys, cardinality, optionality, and temporal rules.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Model and source traceability (`traceability`)

Trace conceptual to logical to physical design and source-to-target mappings and deviations.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Review and approval (`review`)

Record findings and decisions from requirements, design, and physical feasibility reviews.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Version and change control (`change_control`)

For breaking changes, record impact, migration, compatibility, and rollback.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Quality and acceptance evidence (`acceptance_evidence`)

Use scenarios, profiling, and reverse comparison with implementation to verify the model.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.
