## Domain analysis and decisions

### Charter and value (`charter`)

Define governance scope, business value, authority levels, and participating roles.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Decision rights (`decision_rights`)

Assign one final approver for terms, standards, quality, and exceptions.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Policy and standards hierarchy (`policy`)

Distinguish policies, standards, guidance, and procedures with effective-date and change rules.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Ownership and stewardship (`stewardship`)

Define activity-level duties of business owners, stewards, and technical teams.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Issues and exceptions (`exceptions`)

Design intake, adjudication, expiry, compensating controls, and escalation closure.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Glossary governance (`glossary`)

Set versioned proposal, dispute, approval, publication, and retirement steps for terms.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Implementation and scorecard (`scorecard`)

Define verifiable adoption, decision-timeliness, exception, and issue-closure measures.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Approval and acceptance (`acceptance_evidence`)

List the approvals and operating evidence needed for the charter, roles, and workflow to take effect.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.
