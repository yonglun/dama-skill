## Domain analysis and decisions

### Purpose and alternatives (`purpose`)

State the proposed use, necessity, and feasible less-intrusive alternatives.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Data and use boundary (`use_boundary`)

Bound data sources, secondary uses, sharing, and prohibited uses.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Affected parties (`affected_parties`)

Identify groups with different impacts and those not represented.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Benefits harms and uncertainty (`harms`)

Compare benefits, potential harms, evidence gaps, and remaining uncertainty by group.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Controls and evidence (`controls`)

Specify harm reduction, human review, records, monitoring, and independent review.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Recourse (`recourse`)

Explain how affected people can learn, challenge, correct, or opt out.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Conclusion and decision rights (`decision`)

Recommend approval, conditional approval, or stop and name the authorized decision maker.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.


### Review and stop conditions (`stop_conditions`)

Define review and stop triggers for harm, invalidated evidence, or use drift.

> Enter project evidence, conclusion, and owner.
